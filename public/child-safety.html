Link-Ap ⚑ Child Safety Policy

Child Safety Standards

Thapelo Tsiu, developer of Link-Ap, is committed to the safety of all users. This page sets out our binding standards regarding the protection of minors on the Link-Ap platform (link-ap.online).

01

Zero-Tolerance Prohibition

Explicit prohibition of CSAE on Link-Ap

Link-Ap strictly and unconditionally prohibits all forms of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) on its platform. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) — any content that sexually depicts or exploits a minor
  • Grooming — any attempt by an adult to build trust with a minor for the purpose of exploitation
  • Solicitation — requesting sexual content or contact from or involving a minor
  • Trafficking — recruitment, facilitation, or promotion of child trafficking
  • Any content that normalises, glorifies, or minimises harm to children

Any user found to have engaged in CSAE activity on Link-Ap will be permanently banned immediately, reported to the relevant law enforcement authorities, and their content will be reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline where required.

02

Platform Age Requirements

Link-Ap is a platform for adults only

Link-Ap is a professional networking application designed exclusively for users who are 18 years of age or older. Our Terms of Service explicitly prohibit minors from creating accounts or using the Link-Ap platform. We do not knowingly collect or store personal data from anyone under the age of 18.

If we discover that a user is under 18, their account will be terminated immediately and any data associated with the account will be deleted in accordance with applicable law.

03

Reporting Mechanism

In-app and direct reporting channels

Link-Ap provides users with in-app reporting tools to flag inappropriate content or behaviour. Any report that involves a child safety concern is treated as our highest priority and will be reviewed immediately.

Users can also report concerns directly to our child safety point of contact at any time:

04

CSAM Handling & Reporting

How Link-Ap addresses Child Sexual Abuse Material

In the event that CSAM is discovered on the Link-Ap platform, we will:

  • Remove the content immediately and permanently
  • Suspend and ban the responsible account without notice
  • Preserve evidence and report to law enforcement authorities in South Africa (SAPS) and internationally as required
  • Submit a CyberTip report to the NCMEC (National Center for Missing & Exploited Children) CyberTipline
  • Cooperate fully with any subsequent investigation
05

Legal Compliance

Applicable laws and regulations

Thapelo Tsiu, developer of Link-Ap, complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including but not limited to:

  • The Films and Publications Act 65 of 1996 (South Africa), as amended
  • The Children's Act 38 of 2005 (South Africa)
  • The Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), No. 4 of 2013
  • The US PROTECT Our Children Act and applicable NCMEC reporting requirements
  • Any other applicable international child protection legislation
